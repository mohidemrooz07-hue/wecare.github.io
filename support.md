### Common help

- **Phone verification:** Check that the number is correct and can receive SMS. Never share a verification code with support.
- **Notifications:** Check the app’s notification permission in iOS Settings.
- **Restore a Premium purchase:** Open the Premium screen and choose Restore. Apple manages billing and cancellations in Apple Account subscription settings.
- **Delete your account:** Open Account/Profile settings and choose Delete account. Deleting your app account does not cancel an Apple subscription.
- **Report a product issue:** Use Report an Issue in the app or email the support address above.

We + Care is not an emergency service and does not monitor Well Checks as emergency requests. If someone is in immediate danger or needs urgent medical help, contact local emergency services.
